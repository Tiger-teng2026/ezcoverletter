## Step-by-Step: How to Draft a Winning Cover Letter in 5 Minutes

If you want a cover letter that actually gets read, it needs three essential ingredients:

1. **Contextual Relevance**: Mentions specific requirements from the job posting.
2. **Quantifiable Evidence**: Connects your past wins directly to their needs.
3. **Authentic Tone**: Sounds like a driven human, not a corporate robot.

Here is the exact step-by-step formula to craft one in 5 minutes:

### Step 1: Identify the "Core Pain Point" (1 Minute)

Scan the job description for the top 2-3 requirements.

- *Example:* If hiring a Senior Marketing Manager, their pain point might be "Scaling paid ad campaigns while maintaining ROI."
- *Action:* Your cover letter must immediately state how you have solved *that exact problem* before.

### Step 2: Use the 3-Paragraph Winning Structure (1 Minute)

Don't overcomplicate the layout. Keep it lean and impactful:

- **Paragraph 1: The Hook.** Express genuine enthusiasm for the role and summarize your high-level pitch in one sentence.
- **Paragraph 2: The Proof.** Detail 2-3 specific accomplishments with numbers (e.g., *"Increased organic search traffic by 140% in 6 months"*).
- **Paragraph 3: The Call to Action.** Reiterate your fit and invite them to a quick chat.
